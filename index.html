What Ultron can do

One model, four jobs. Hover a card to see it light up.

Forecast any hour

Give Ultron the hour, ambient temperature, humidity and solar light. It returns the expected demand in kW.

Switch the grid

Above 160 kW, or any time from 18:00 to 22:00, Ultron moves the load to renewable storage. Otherwise it stays on the main grid.

Raise the alarm

At 200 kW or more, Ultron flags a critical overload in red, whatever the grid mode.

Plan extra energy

Need more power at a certain hour? Enter the amount and the reason, and Ultron re-checks the grid mode and the battery.

Ask Ultron

Pick the hour and describe the weather. The forecast button rises as you arrive.
